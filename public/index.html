About

The art of scroll-driven storytelling.

Inspired by the cinematic product pages of Apple, Tesla, and other industry leaders, ScrollScrub turns ordinary videos into immersive, scroll-driven experiences. Every pixel of movement reveals a new frame, giving your audience complete control over the narrative pace.



Built with Node.js, Express, and ffmpeg — it's lightweight, fast, and entirely self-hosted. Drop in your video, and the server handles the rest.
